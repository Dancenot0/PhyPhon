Controles y formulariosControls and forms

Widgets listos para reunir y mostrar datos del usuario. Todos heredan de Widget, por lo que soportan animate(), color, opacidad y eventos conectables.Ready-to-use widgets to collect and show user data. All inherit from Widget and therefore support animate(), colour, opacity and connectable events.

Validación de formularioForm validation

Reglas declarativas sobre campos: obligatorio, longitud mínima/máxima, email, etc. Se validan en lote en MobileApp o con FormValidator de core/form_validator.py.Declarative rules over fields: required, min/max length, email and more, validated in a batch by FormValidator in core/form_validator.py.

from pyphon.core.form_validator import FormValidator
formulario = FormValidator()
formulario.add_field("user", user_input,
                     [("required",), ("min_length", 3)])
formulario.add_field("email", email_input,
                     [("required",), ("email",)])
if formulario.validate():
    print("todo correcto")
