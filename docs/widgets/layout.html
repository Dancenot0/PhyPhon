Contenedores y vistasContainers and views

Cada contenedor hereda de Widget y organiza a sus add_child(). Eliges ancho, alto, espaciado y padding; los hijos se reparten según la política del layout.Every container inherits from Widget and lays out its children through add_child(). You choose width, height, spacing and padding; children are arranged by the container's policy.

Cajas y rejillas

ClaseCómo reparte los hijosHow children are arranged
VerticalLayout / HorizontalLayoutColumna o fila con espaciado uniforme.Column or row with even spacing.
BoxLayoutBase genérica; acepta orientación.Generic base; accepts an orientation.
LinearLayout / FrameLayout / RelativeLayoutFamilia lineal, superpuesta o por anclas relativas.Linear, stacked or anchor-relative family.
GridLayout(columns=N)Malla de N columnas.Grid of N columns.
TableLayout / ConstraintLayout / CoordinatorLayoutFilas/celdas, restricciones y coordinación de overlays.Rows/cells, constraints and overlay coordination.
from pyphon.widgets import VerticalLayout, HorizontalLayout, GridLayout, FrameLayout, RelativeLayout, ConstraintLayout, CoordinatorLayout, BoxLayout, LinearLayout, TableLayout

root = VerticalLayout(width=360, height=640, spacing=14, padding=16)
root.background_color = Palette.BACKGROUND

fila = HorizontalLayout(spacing=10)
fila.add_widget(Button(text="A"))
fila.add_widget(Button(text="B"))
root.add_widget(fila)

malla = GridLayout(columns=2, spacing=12, padding=16)
malla.add_widget(Card())     # los hijos se van llenando por celdas
malla.add_widget(Card())
root.add_widget(malla)

ClaseUso típicoTypical use

from pyphon.widgets import StatusBar, Toolbar, BottomNavigationView, BottomNavItem, TabLayout, TabItem, DrawerLayout, Breadcrumb

cromo = VerticalLayout(width=360, height=640)
cromo.add_widget(StatusBar(width=360))
cromo.add_widget(Toolbar(title="Tienda"))
cromo.add_widget(DrawerLayout(width=360, height=440,
                              drawer_width=280))
